# 2. Purpose of This Manual
This manual teaches you how to use CarHunter to find car deals, check that they are real, and get them ready for the owner to approve. By the end you will be able to work the deal feed, verify a vehicle's facts and values, message sellers, review auction cars, and build a clean owner packet - even if you have never bought a car before.
Read it in order the first week. After that, keep it open and jump to the section you need.


# 3. What CarHunter Does
CarHunter is a system that watches car listings all day and pulls the best ones into one place so you do not have to search by hand. The screen you will use is the web app called APEX Deal Radar.

## What the system does for you

- Holds leads - every car it finds shows up as a card in the feed.
- Shows vehicle data - year, make, model, price, mileage, title, photos.
- Shows values - JD Clean Trade and KBB (pulled from Laser Appraiser automatically).
- Scores deals - a 0-100 Deal Score and an estimated profit for each car.
- Flags HOT deals - cars priced about $1,000+ under both books show under Contact Now.
- Tracks status and notes - you can save cars, add notes, and mark where each one stands.
- Helps you review auction run lists (done with the worksheet in Section 10 for now).
- Helps you build owner packets so the owner can decide fast.
- Keeps the business record - the feed is the source of truth.
> ⚠️ Be honest about what is automatic vs manual. The app AUTO-shows JD Clean Trade + KBB, Deal Score, profit, and HOT. JD Full Retail, Base MMR, Carfax history, and Car-Part repair prices are looked up by YOU in outside tools (Sections 10-13). Auction run lists and owner packets are done by hand today.


# 4. Your Role - The Second Line of Defense
The software is the first line: it finds opportunities fast. You are the second line: you make sure the deal is REAL before anyone spends money. The owner is the final line: the owner approves every purchase and every auction bid.

- The app can be wrong or missing facts. Your job is to check reality - VIN, title, miles, condition, history.
- Never guess. If a fact is missing, mark it MISSING and go find it.
- Never approve a buy or a bid yourself unless the owner has given you written permission.
- Every car you touch must end with a clear next step and notes.
> **Example:** The app shows a 2018 Accord, $4,000, profit +$13,000, HOT. That looks amazing - too amazing. You check and the description says 'blown engine, salvage title.' That is why you verify before the owner ever sees a 'great deal.'

## Save, note, set status

- Tap the heart to save a car to your Watchlist.
- Add a note with the facts you verified (see Section 16 for good vs bad notes).
- Set the status so everyone knows where the car stands.
- Note: saves, notes, and status are stored in YOUR browser. Use the same computer/profile each shift, and follow the owner-packet step (Section 15) so important deals are recorded for the owner, not just on your machine.

## Understanding scores and risk

- Deal Score (0-100): higher = better deal. It blends profit, how far under book, freshness, mileage fit, and title.
- Est profit: the rough money in the deal (value minus price minus repair and fees). Verify it - it uses auto values.
- HOT: about $1,000+ under both books. Strong, but still verify.
- Risk to watch: branded/salvage title, very high miles, or a price that looks too good (often means damage or a scam).


# 8. Private-Party Lead Workflow (one car, start to finish)

1. Open the lead (click the card).
2. Check the vehicle facts: year, make, model, trim, price, location.
3. Check the VIN: is it present and 17 characters? Copy it. (No VIN on a good car = ask the seller.)
4. Check the mileage: does it fit the price and year? Very low or blank miles = verify.
5. Check the title: clean, or branded/salvage/rebuilt? Branded changes the value a lot.
6. Read the full description: look for mechanic-special words (Section listing below).
7. Review the values: JD Clean Trade + KBB show in the app. Look up JD Full Retail + Base MMR in Laser (Section 11).
8. Check history if needed: run Carfax (Section 12) for title brand, accidents, mileage, owners.
9. Identify gaps: anything missing or that doesn't match - mark MISSING.
10. Contact the seller using a script (Section 14).
11. Log the reply with the facts the seller gives.
12. Escalate to the owner if it's strong/risky/over your limit (Section 15).

## Mechanic-special words to catch in the description
needs engine, bad engine, blown motor, knocking, head gasket, overheating, needs transmission, no reverse, slipping, won't start, doesn't run, salvage, rebuilt, branded title, flood, frame damage, mechanic special, project, as-is.

## Checklist - private-party lead

- [ ] Facts checked
- [ ] VIN present + copied
- [ ] Mileage makes sense
- [ ] Title known
- [ ] Description read for issues
- [ ] Values reviewed (JD Clean/KBB + Laser Retail/MMR)
- [ ] Carfax run if needed
- [ ] Gaps marked MISSING
- [ ] Seller contacted
- [ ] Reply logged
- [ ] Escalated if needed; next action set

## What to do if this happens

- No VIN: ask the seller for it before spending more time. Still none on a high-value car? Escalate.
- Title is branded/salvage: flag it, use the lower (0.70) value basis, and escalate.
- Price is way below book (under ~35% of book): treat as a red flag - ask hard condition/title questions; likely damage or scam.
- Seller won't answer questions or rushes you: stay polite, log it, and escalate if it's a strong car.
> **Example:** 2016 RAV4, $5,000, clean title, 80k mi. JD Clean $10,833, KBB $10,892 in the app. You copy the VIN, run Carfax (clean, 1 owner, no accidents), message the seller (Script 1+3), they confirm title in hand. You log it and build an owner packet - strong, verified, ready.

| Transmission room | Money left if you replace the TRANSMISSION only |

## The most important rule: engine and transmission are SEPARATE scenarios
> ⚠️ Never add engine repair AND transmission repair on the same car unless the owner asks for a worst-case review. Price the ENGINE scenario by itself, and the TRANSMISSION scenario by itself.

**Engine room (engine scenario)**
Engine room = expected exit value - auction fees - transport - title/admin - standard recon - engine part (Car-Part 84101) - $1,000 engine labor - diagnostic reserve - unknown-risk reserve - target profit. If engine room is at or below $0, recommend PASS.

**Transmission room (transmission scenario)**
Transmission room = expected exit value - auction fees - transport - title/admin - standard recon - transmission part (Car-Part 84101) - $1,000 transmission labor - diagnostic reserve - unknown-risk reserve - target profit.
Your recommended MAX BID is at or below the room for the problem the car actually has. If a fact is missing, mark it MISSING - do not guess a value.

# 12. Carfax / Vehicle-History Workflow
History tells you if the car's past matches the seller's story. Use the VIN.

1. Confirm the VIN (17 characters; matches the listing).
2. Run the history report (Carfax or the approved tool).
3. Check the title/brand: clean, salvage, rebuilt, flood, lemon?
4. Check accidents/damage.
5. Check the mileage history for rollback or gaps.
6. Check owners and use (personal, fleet, rental).
7. Compare to the seller's story - do they match?
8. Grade the history and add notes to CarHunter.

## History grade (simple)

| Grade | Means |
|---|---|
| A | Clean title, no accidents, consistent miles, 1-2 owners. |
| B | Clean title, minor issue (small accident or fleet use) - usually fine, note it. |
| C | Clean title but real concerns (multiple accidents, odd miles) - escalate. |
| Red | Branded/salvage/flood/lemon title, rollback, or major damage - escalate; price on the low basis. |

> [SCREENSHOT NEEDED — Carfax / history report summary]  
> - Where: Carfax (or approved tool), after running a real VIN (VERIFY LIVE).  
> - What to notice: Title brand line, accident count, mileage history, number of owners.  
> - What to click next: Write the grade (A/B/C/Red) + key facts into the car's notes.  
> - Common mistake here: Trusting the seller over the report - the report wins; log any mismatch.  
> **Example:** Seller says 'never wrecked, one owner.' Carfax shows 2 accidents and 3 owners. That's a mismatch - grade C, note it, and tell the owner before anyone drives out.


# 13. Car-Part Workflow (repair part prices)
Car-Part.com tells you what a used engine or transmission really costs locally. Use it for mechanic specials and auction cars.

1. Go to https://www.car-part.com/index.html.
2. Search using ZIP 84101 (our local search ZIP).
3. Pick the year/make/model and choose the part: Engine (search engines).
4. Find the cheapest MATCHING part (right engine size/fitment).
5. Record: price, vendor name, vendor location, the part's mileage, warranty, and today's date.
6. Repeat for the Transmission (search transmissions).
7. Note any fitment concerns (wrong engine size, sub-model differences).
8. Put the engine price into the ENGINE scenario and the transmission price into the TRANSMISSION scenario (keep them separate - Section 10).

> [SCREENSHOT NEEDED — Car-Part.com search results (ZIP 84101)]  
> - Where: car-part.com, after searching a real engine or transmission with ZIP 84101.  
> - What to notice: The results list: price, yard/vendor, location/distance, the part's mileage, and warranty.  
> - What to click next: Pick the cheapest part that truly fits; copy its details into the worksheet.  
> - Common mistake here: Grabbing a cheaper part that is the wrong engine size/fitment.  

## What to do if this happens

- No Utah/local result: widen the search area or note 'no local part - shipping needed' and use the next closest; mark confidence Low.
- Prices vary a lot: use a mid price for the math, keep the high one as a safety check.
- Don't forget core charge + shipping in the part cost.


# 14. Seller Messaging (scripts)
Keep messages short, polite, and local-sounding. Ask for the appointment quickly. Ask title + condition clearly. No pressure, no false claims. Log EVERY message you send and EVERY reply.

**First message**
> Hi, is your {year} {make} {model} still available? I'm a local buyer and can come take a look today or tomorrow if that works.

**Ask VIN**
> Could you share the VIN? I just want to check the details/history before I drive out so I don't waste your time.

**Ask title**
> Is the title clean and in hand, or is it branded/salvage?

**Ask condition**
> How long have you owned it, and is there anything wrong that's not in the listing - mechanical, accidents, warning lights?

**Ask engine issue**
> I understand it may have engine trouble - that's okay, I buy those. Does it start at all, any knocking or overheating?

**Ask transmission issue**
> Does it drive and shift okay? Does reverse work, any slipping?

**Follow-up**
> Hey, just following up on the {make} {model} - still available? Happy to come look whenever works.

**Polite pass**
> Thanks for the details and your time - it's not the right fit for me right now. Good luck with the sale!

## Logging rule

- Log each message you send (with time).
- Log each reply with the facts: ownership, issues, title status, VIN, why selling.
- If they go quiet, follow up once, then set Watch or Dead with a reason.

## What each status means

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| New | Just arrived; not worked yet. |
| Triage | You're reviewing it now. |
| Need VIN | Good car but VIN missing - asking seller. |

# 17. Troubleshooting - What To Do If This Happens

**CarHunter won't load**
Refresh (F5). Try another browser. Check internet. Down more than ~15 min? Tell your manager and work your existing leads/follow-ups meanwhile.

**Feed is not updating (no new cars)**
Confirm the Laser tab/value bridge is running (Section 11). If still nothing new for 30+ min, tell your manager - it may be the scraper or the Bright Data token.

**A lead is missing data**
Fill what you can from Laser/Carfax/the listing. Mark anything you can't confirm as MISSING. Don't guess.

**Values are missing on every car**
The Laser tab is closed or the session expired. Open/refresh the logged-in Laser tab. Still blank? Tell your manager.

**Laser login fails**
Re-check the login; is the subscription active? Try once more. Unresolved? Escalate to the owner - don't guess values.

**Carfax unavailable**
Note 'history not available' and proceed carefully; don't present unverified history as clean. Escalate big deals.

**Auction file won't open/parse**
Work it by hand into the worksheet (Section 10). Note which rows were manual.

**Car-Part has no Utah result**
Widen the area or note 'no local part - shipping needed,' use the next closest, mark confidence Low, raise the unknown-risk reserve.

**Score looks wrong**
Check the values + trim/mileage (wrong trim = wrong value). If values are right but the score seems off, tell your manager.

**Seller changes their story**
Log exactly what changed. Trust the history/VIN over the story. Escalate before any money moves.

**Owner is unreachable**
Follow the owner's standing rule. If there is none and it's a buy/bid decision, do NOT commit - wait.

**You made a mistake**
Stop. Log exactly what happened and tell your manager right away. Never hide it - fixing fast is what matters.
