Ledger Rules

These rules govern every calculation in this report. They are applied consistently to all 24 recorded ledger events.

  • Positive balance means Z owes Mo.
  • Negative balance means Mo owes Z.
  • Partner deals are split 50 / 50 unless otherwise stated.
  • Only expenses labeled “Mo” are counted as Mo expenses. All other expenses are treated as Z-paid unless the ledger specifically says another person paid them.
  • Z-paid purchase funding is always written as: “Z paid for Mo’s 50% share of the purchase.”
  • Named payees/vendors (Drako, Tito, Taylor, Talon, Luis, Angela, AutoZone, gas, tow, Lyft, emissions, car wash, parts, repairs, etc.) are listed exactly as written and classified clearly. If a payer is not explicitly Mo, it is never counted as a Mo expense.
